Landscape processes and sustainability

GEOGRAPHY · LANDSCAPE · SUSTAINABILITY

Qingxu Huang

I am a Professor and Deputy Dean of the School of Natural Resources, Faculty of Geographical Science, at Beijing Normal University.

My research focuses on urban and rural landscape processes and sustainability. I investigate how land-use change shapes ecosystems, biodiversity and human well-being, and how this understanding can inform the planning and design of more sustainable landscapes.

Research interests

  1. 01

    Landscape dynamics

    Monitoring, analyzing and modeling land-use and land-cover change across urban and rural landscapes.

  2. 02

    Ecosystem services

    Understanding how landscape change affects ecosystem services, biodiversity and human well-being.

  3. 03

    Urban & rural sustainability

    Connecting landscape processes with sustainable development, spatial planning and environmental decision-making.

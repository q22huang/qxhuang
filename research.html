Landscape processes and sustainability

RESEARCH & PROJECTS

Research

Understanding changing landscapes, and their consequences for people and nature.

Research themesProjects as principal investigatorCollaborative projects

Research themes

  1. 01

    Landscape dynamics

    Monitoring, analyzing and modeling land-use and land-cover change across urban and rural landscapes.

  2. 02

    Ecosystem services

    Understanding how landscape change affects ecosystem services, biodiversity and human well-being.

  3. 03

    Urban & rural sustainability

    Connecting landscape processes with sustainable development, spatial planning and environmental decision-making.

My work combines statistical and spatial analysis, social surveys, land-use models, econometrics and industrial ecology. Research connects observations and simulation with questions about the environmental and social consequences of urbanization.
